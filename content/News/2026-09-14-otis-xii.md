## Please welcome CJ Quines as the first OTIS staff

I'm happy to announce that [CJ Quines](https://cjquines.com)
will officially be joining OTIS as staff for the 2026--2027 school year.
After twelve years of OTIS, I'm _finally_ starting to grow the team
from the one-person show that it's been up until now.

I think this is the part where I'm supposed to introduce the new staff.
CJ is also a math+CS major from MIT (and also
[✈✈✈ Galactic Trendsetters ✈✈✈](https://galacticpuzzlehunt.com)),
and was
[an avid blogger for MIT Admissions](https://mitadmissions.org/blogs/author/cjq/).
The first paragraph of CJ's biography on MIT's site goes:

> What does it take to get to know someone?
> I've written three hundred thousand words in these blog posts,
> and I don't think that's enough.
> I'll save you the effort to learn the basics, at least:
> my name is CJ, I graduated in 2023, I grew up in the Philippines,
> and while in MIT, I lived in East Campus.

If you've been following OTIS closely,
you might have seen that CJ helped me to set the
[OPAL 3 hunt for OTIS](https://web.evanchen.cc/opal.html).
(As of yesterday, that hunt is now publicly posted, if you want to try it!)
Now that CJ is officially signed on,
you can expect to see more of CJ's work over the year.

## The Global Talent Fund is the first OTIS sponsor

At the same time, I'm happy to announce that I've
been appointed as a STEM Talent Fellow by the
[Global Talent Fund](https://www.globtalent.org/).
I'm grateful for the GTF's support,
which was part of what made it possible to finally make my first hire.

If you're an organization helping run the pipeline for your country to IMO/IOI,
and you're looking for support,
the [GTF Olympiad Grants](https://www.globtalent.org/gtf-olympiad-grants)
may be worth looking at.
(Their next open call for applications is due 15 Oct 2026.)
